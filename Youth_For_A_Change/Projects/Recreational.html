Recreational Hall

The recreational hall project will provide a safe space for youth to engage in various activities such as sports, arts, and community events. This facility is designed to foster social interaction, physical health, and personal development.

Sports Facilities: Equipped with courts and fields for basketball, soccer, and other sports. These facilities encourage physical activity and teamwork among youth.

Arts and Crafts: Offering workshops and classes in painting, sculpture, and other creative arts. These programs nurture creativity and provide a constructive outlet for self-expression.

Community Events: Hosting events such as talent shows, cultural festivals, and educational seminars. These events bring the community together and provide enriching experiences for participants.

Join us in supporting our youth by creating a space where they can learn, grow, and thrive. Together, we can make a positive impact on their lives!
