Study Hall (Under Construction)

As part of our Youth Outreach Program, we are constructing a dedicated study hall designed to provide a safe, structured environment where students can focus on their academic growth. Once completed, this space will offer much-needed academic support, including tutoring, mentorship, and access to educational resources like textbooks, computers, and internet connectivity.

The study hall will function as more than just a quiet space for learning—it will be a hub for personal development. Students will have the opportunity to engage in one-on-one mentorship sessions that help them navigate academic challenges, set personal goals, and develop essential life skills. Through workshops, group activities, and peer support, they will gain confidence in their abilities and build a strong foundation for their future success.

By keeping students engaged after school and providing a positive, focused environment, the study hall will not only contribute to academic achievement but also help steer youth away from negative influences. We believe that this space will empower students to take control of their education, stay on track with their studies, and grow into confident, motivated individuals.
