YouthForAChange Projects

Community Garden

Our community garden project provides fresh produce and a place for residents to connect with nature. It's a space for learning and growing, promoting sustainable practices and healthy eating.

Health and Wellness Programs

We offer various health and wellness programs focused on improving mental and physical health. These programs include workshops, fitness classes, and mental health support groups.

Expanding Schools and Educational Materials

This project aims to increase the size of schools and provide necessary educational materials to students. We believe in creating an inclusive and effective learning environment for all.

Recreational Hall

Our recreational hall offers a safe space for youth to engage in sports, arts, and other recreational activities. It's a hub for community interaction and personal development.
